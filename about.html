About Us

A local fireworks shop, right here in Cardston — serving our community with quality products and friendly service.

Who We Are

Chief Mountain Fireworks is a local fireworks shop located right here in Cardston, Alberta — inside Moccasin Flats Plaza at Unit 5, home of Chief Mountain Pharmacy. We’re proud to be an authorized local retailer of quality, family-safe fireworks, supplied by Blast-Off Fireworks.

We brought Chief Mountain Fireworks to Cardston because our community deserves access to quality, safe, and legal fireworks right in their own backyard — without having to drive out of town or wait on a package. Whether you’re celebrating Canada Day, a birthday, a graduation, or just want to light up a summer evening with family, we’ve got you covered.

We carry everything in-store — come in, browse at your own pace, and our friendly team will help you pick the right products for your occasion and answer any safety questions you have. No online ordering, no shipping — just good old-fashioned local service.

What We Stand For

Community First

We’re your neighbors. We live and work in this community and we want to see everyone celebrating safely and enjoying themselves.

In-Store, In Person

We don’t ship. Come in and we’ll take care of you face to face — the way local shopping should be.

Safe & Legal Only

Every product we sell is legal for consumer use in Alberta. We follow all provincial and local safety regulations and we’re happy to talk through safe use with any customer.

Quality You Can Trust

Every product we carry is consumer-safe, pre-packaged, and legal for use in Alberta. We stand behind what we sell — quality you can count on.

Come See Us

We’re in-store only — no delivery or shipping available. Stop by at Moccasin Flats Plaza in Cardston, or give us a call if you have a question before you visit.
